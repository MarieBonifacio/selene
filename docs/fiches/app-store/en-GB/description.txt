Selene is a research notebook for long writing projects: a thesis, a book, a series of articles. It keeps your fragments, your sources and your hypotheses linked, and brings back what you had forgotten, right up to the last chapter.

THE TEXT, CHAPTER BY CHAPTER
• A word count, its goal and its pace; each chapter with its progress.
• Fragments attached to their chapter; an edited version keeps the old one readable underneath.
• "I'm stopping here…": your next step, noted as you leave, found again when you return.

SOURCES, LINKED
• A link or a DOI is enough: title, authors, journal and date fill themselves in.
• Each source links to the fragment it documents.
• Your Zotero library, read-only.
• BibTeX and CSL-JSON export, for LaTeX, Zotero, Zettlr or Pandoc.

THINKING WITH WHAT YOU WROTE
• A status for each idea: observed, hypothesis, interpretation, unexplained.
• Links between entries: derives from, contradicts, echoes, documents.
• Open tensions: two ideas that contradict each other, waiting for a synthesis.
• The weekly review: ten minutes with what sleeps, what contradicts itself and what awaits its evidence.
• Search covers the whole notebook, statuses and dates included.

COME AND GO FREELY
• Your Markdown notes from Obsidian or Zettlr import, [[…]] links included.
• Everything exports, at any time: a full backup, Markdown, a folder ready to reread.

WHAT SELENE DOES NOT DO
• No ads, no trackers. Nothing measures what you write.
• No account required: Selene works entirely on your device, even offline. An account only serves to find your notebook on your other devices.
• No artificial intelligence forced on you.

The code is public. Privacy policy: https://mariebonifacio.github.io/selene/privacy.html
